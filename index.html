Good to know

A few common questions.

Still wondering about something? I’m always happy to chat before you book.

Do I need an instrument at home?+

Piano students need regular access to a piano or full-size weighted keyboard. If you’re choosing one, I’m happy to offer guidance.

What age can students begin?+

Readiness varies, but many piano students begin around age six. Voice lessons are generally best suited to teens and adults.

Do adult beginners need experience?+

Not at all. Adult beginners and returning musicians are warmly welcome, and lessons move at a comfortable, practical pace.

Are performance opportunities available?+

Yes. Optional studio gatherings and recitals give students a welcoming way to share what they’ve learned.
